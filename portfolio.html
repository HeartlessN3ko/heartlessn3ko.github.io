I Build Things Because I Have To.

Indie game developer, systems designer, and composer with 20+ years in music production and commercial game credits. I design, build, and ship complete systems — code, sound, and world — as a solo developer. Currently building toward a small indie studio focused on deep, meaningful game experiences.


39 years old. Black trans woman. I bring that perspective into everything I make — the worlds I build, the systems I design, and the communities I create for.


I use AI tools the way a good producer uses gear — strategically, intentionally, and always in service of the work. AI handles what it's good at. The vision, the design, the soul of the project stays mine.


Revenue share over upfront pay. Small tight crew over big loud teams. If you make things because you have to, we'll get along fine.
