Core Expectations

Safety, order, purpose.

These directives are the website version of the in-Discord Information Center. Once applicants read this here, Discord onboarding can be shorter and more in-character.

01

Respect other players

No harassment, hate speech, targeted cruelty, or pressure. In-character conflict is welcome. Out-of-character harm is not.

02

Consent matters

Do not force romance, trauma, injury, mind control, death, or major character consequences on another player without clear agreement.

03

Keep powers playable

Strong characters are allowed. Unbounded characters are not. Give abilities limits, weaknesses, costs, and room for other players to matter.

04

Respect SRX intake

Arrival, dampening, confiscation, and registration are part of the opening experience. They bring wildly different characters into the same playable world.

05

AI NPCs are not permission slips

If an AI response gets weird, too intense, or contradicts staff direction, staff direction wins. Report issues instead of exploiting them. Cute loopholes are still loopholes. Yes, even that one.

06

Keep staff-only secrets private

Hidden powers, GM hooks, private backstory, and content limits are for review and story planning. They should not appear in public bios unless you choose to reveal them.

07

Use content limits clearly

List hard limits and topics you want avoided. Staff will use this during review and future event planning.

08

Approval comes before entry

Submitting an application does not grant Discord access. Approval triggers the bot DM with invite instructions and next steps.
