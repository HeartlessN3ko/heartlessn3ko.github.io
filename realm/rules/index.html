SRX Civil Transfer Authority // Receiving-World Law

Peace survives the crossing.

Every person brought through an SRX Gateway enters under the same protections and obligations. This acknowledgment will be sealed to an unverified identity.

Identity anchor: unverified
Transit Covenant

Safety, order, purpose.

Terra receives soldiers, civilians, sovereigns, machines, spirits, and beings without local classification. These directives preserve personhood while preventing a frightened arrival—or an unstable power—from becoming another catastrophe.

01

Every person retains personhood

Origin, species, body, faith, allegiance, and capability do not reduce anyone's right to safety. Harassment, dehumanization, hate speech, and targeted cruelty are prohibited in every SRX-controlled channel and facility.

02

Sovereignty requires consent

No traveler may impose romance, psychic influence, bodily alteration, imprisonment, severe injury, or death upon another without clear and continuing agreement. Power does not erase consent.

03

Declare dangerous capabilities

High-output abilities are not grounds for rejection by themselves. Concealing unstable magic, autonomous weapons, contagious conditions, involuntary transformations, or catastrophic thresholds is.

04

Comply with arrival stabilization

Gateway assessment, temporary dampening, hazardous-material custody, medical screening, and identity confirmation protect both the traveler and the receiving population. Interference may suspend transfer authorization.

05

Malformed system output grants no authority

ORACLE and ARIA assist SRX personnel, but corrupted or contradictory output does not supersede a lawful Command directive. Report anomalies. Do not exploit them. Yes, SRX has met clever people before.

06

Restricted knowledge remains restricted

Sealed histories, covert affiliations, medical disclosures, and security-sensitive capabilities are released only by their owner or lawful authority. Access to a secret is not permission to expose it.

07

Declare psychological and medical limits

Identify subjects, procedures, environments, or contact that may cause harm. Receiving personnel use these limits when assigning quarters, examinations, escorts, and future operations.

08

Authorization precedes transit

Pre-registration does not open a Gateway. SRX Command must confirm that the traveler, receiving station, and destination conditions are ready before dimensional transfer begins.
