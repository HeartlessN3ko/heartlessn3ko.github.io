THE QUESTION

CAN A SMALL TEAM
MAKE A GREAT GAME?

Can a three-person AI-native development team create a genuinely good game in 30 days?

AI-assisted development is expected. Bring your preferred AI coding and development tools. The finished games should be judged as games, not just as something “good for an AI game.”

We’ll document the development process and final results to examine whether AI-assisted game development actually scales across small teams.

WHAT’S TAKING SHAPE

  • Form teams of three
  • Recruit teammates through the community
  • Use your preferred AI development tools
  • Build for 30 days
  • Publish and devlog your own journey
  • Submit a playable game for community judging

The community will play and vote on the finished games. The winning team will nominate an MVP to receive $100 credit toward the AI service or platform of their choice.

Full rules, dates, judging methodology and submission requirements are coming soon.
