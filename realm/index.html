Realm Basics

The Nexus Realm does not behave like a single universe.

Terra sits between countless realities. Cultures, species, technologies, and magic systems have crossed into this world for centuries. Diversity is not unusual here. It is the foundation modern civilization was built on.

The Nexus Realm

Dimensional boundaries have weakened throughout recorded history, allowing fragments of foreign worlds, civilizations, and individuals to cross into Terra. Some arrived accidentally. Others were brought here intentionally. Many never knew they crossed at all.

Terra

Ancient ruins stand beside modern cities. Advanced technology exists alongside forgotten magic. Species once separated by impossible distances now call the same neighborhoods home.

For most refugees, your journey begins in Midgar.

The Rift Events

Entire civilizations and landscapes once appeared without warning. The Age of Mythology gave way to the First Age, then the Second Age: eras of heroes, governments, institutions, and uneasy peace.

The Third Age

For the first time in recorded history, the Rift has been stabilized. SRX detects collapsing dimensions and establishes controlled evacuation corridors before total collapse occurs.
