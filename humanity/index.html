How the Codex works
Book II The Twelve Laws of Humanity The twelve concise principles at the center of the Codex: truth, life, strength, freedom, community, plurality, repair, knowledge, experience, future possibility, nature, and stewardship. Placement in review The Purpose of Humanity Receive the inheritance, experience your life, add what you can, and pass it forward. Book XV The Human Continuum Humanity across generations: memory, knowledge, failure, legacy, and future possibility. Book XVI Human Sovereignty Meaningful authority over body, identity, belief, relationships, direction, and future. Book XVII The Human Experience Connection, creation, play, nature, knowledge, ritual, grief, purpose, and wonder. Book XVIII The Human Spirit A disciplined home for mystery, belief, spiritual experience, and the sacred answer: we do not know.
